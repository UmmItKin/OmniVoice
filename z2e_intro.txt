Introducing Z2E, Zero to Exploit.

A fully autonomous AI security agent for CTF and pentesting.
One command in, full workflow out: read, run, analyze, solve.
From discovery to exploitation, all in one terminal.

## Voice Style
- **Energy**: High enthusiasm, lively and engaging
- **Tone**: Warm but professional, like telling someone about something amazing
- **Rhythm**: Natural pauses, emphasis on key words, slight variation in pitch
- **Attitude**: Confident, proud, slightly dramatic but genuine
- **Pacing**: Moderate, not rushed, let important words breathe
